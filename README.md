## Hi, I'm Manseerat Kaur Aulakh 👋

Undergraduate researcher in machine learning · B.Tech CSE (3rd year) at **Amity University Punjab**, Mohali, India · working with Dr. Vishwanath Bijalwan.

I study **the geometry of learned representations**: how it breaks under compression and distillation, when it can be repaired, and how geometric signals such as curvature can make retrieval-augmented generation and physics-informed models more reliable.

### 🔬 Research interests
- Representation learning and embedding geometry
- Model compression and post-hoc repair
- Geometric methods for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
- Interpretable, physics-informed networks (Kolmogorov–Arnold and Hamiltonian)
